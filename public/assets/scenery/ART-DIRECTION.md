# Toy-On category illustrations

Generated with the built-in image generation tool, using the existing winter.webp, garden.webp and twilight.webp scenes as references. Original scenery and product assets are preserved. Final generated artwork is WebP encoded at quality 86 without resizing.

## Shared prompt

Create a polished flat 2D illustrated landscape background for the Toy-On celebration website, using the attached scene only as composition/subject reference. Transform it into art-directed playful editorial screen-print / cut-paper illustration with clean graphic silhouettes, broad color areas, gentle paper grain, layered flat terrain, imaginative but restrained details. Absolutely no 3D rendering, clay shapes, glossy shading, bevels, photorealism, blur or volumetric light. Landscape aspect ratio 3:2. The design will be cropped inside a large letter O; put interesting trees/landforms across the upper band and left/right/lower perimeter, with a quieter central open area. No O, no text, no logo, no road, no balloons, no products, no people. Full bleed opaque image, not a mockup, no border.
